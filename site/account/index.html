CHECK YOUR PLAN

Your license is verified entirely offline, so the fastest check is the CLI:

cpass license status

It prints your plan, the account it is issued to, and the expiry — no network call, ever.

LOST YOUR ACTIVATION COMMAND?

Enter the email on your subscription. We email a fresh cpass license activate command and a link to the billing portal. The token is never shown in this page or its address.

Enter the email on your subscription.

An active subscription gets a mail within a minute. A cancelled one is declined — resubscribe from pricing.

MANAGE OR CANCEL BILLING

Billing runs on Stripe. The reissue email above carries a customer-portal link where you can update your card, download invoices, or cancel. Cancelling keeps your token working through its grace period — a network blip never locks your Vault.
